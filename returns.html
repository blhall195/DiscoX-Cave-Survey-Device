Cancellations & returns.

The DiscoX cancellations and returns policy for online orders.

Before it ships

Cancel any time before it's dispatched, for any reason. Full refund.

After delivery

14 days to change your mind, then 14 days to send it back.

Faulty?

I'll repair, replace or refund it, and pay the postage.

Cancelling before it ships

Orders are dispatched by DHL within 2 working days of payment. Until your DiscoX has been handed to DHL you can cancel at any time, for any reason: email info@discox.co.uk with your order reference. I'll refund everything you paid — the device, shipping, and any import taxes you prepaid — to the card you paid with, within 14 days of you asking.

If I can't dispatch within 2 working days I'll tell you as soon as I know, and you can cancel for a full refund.

Changing your mind after delivery

You have 14 days to cancel, starting the day after your DiscoX is delivered to you (or to someone you nominated). This is your right under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, and I offer the same to business and club customers too.

To cancel, tell me before the 14 days are up. An email to info@discox.co.uk with your order reference is enough; you can use the form at the bottom of this page, but you don't have to. You don't need to give a reason.

Then send it back within 14 days of telling me. Email first and I'll send you the return address. Please:

  • Return the device with everything it came with, packed well enough to survive the trip.
  • Outside the UK, ask me for a DHL returns label. That way I can arrange the right re-import paperwork, so no taxes are charged as it comes back into the UK. I'll tell you the cost before I book it, and take it off your refund.
  • Inside the UK, post it back to me with a tracked service and send me the tracking information.

You pay for sending it back when you've changed your mind. If the device is faulty or not as described, I pay for sending it back instead.

Your refund

When you cancel within the 14 days, I'll refund you within 14 days of the device reaching me, or of you sending me proof that you've posted it back, whichever is sooner, to the card you paid with. The refund covers:

  • The device, in full.
  • The delivery charge you paid to get it to you, up to the cost of the cheapest delivery option I offered you. If you chose a faster service (such as Express Worldwide instead of Economy Select), the difference isn't refunded. (The cost of sending it back isn't included — see above.)
  • Import taxes you prepaid to me at checkout, in full.

Import taxes you paid to DHL on delivery went to your own country's customs, not to me, so I can't refund them. Many countries let you reclaim duty and VAT on goods you send back — DHL or your customs authority can tell you how.

Handling it is fine. You can unbox it, switch it on, take readings and try the app, just as you could in a shop. If it comes back damaged, missing parts, or clearly used for real surveying, I may reduce the refund to cover the loss in value. I'll always explain any deduction.

If something is wrong with it

Every DiscoX is calibrated and bench-tested before it leaves, but if yours is faulty, damaged in transit, or not as described, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 protects you, and I pay for sending it back to me (and for getting a repaired or replacement device to you):

  • Within 30 days of delivery, you can reject it for a full refund.
  • After that, I'll repair or replace it. If that doesn't fix the problem, you can have a refund (which may be reduced for the use you've had, if it's more than six months after delivery).
  • For the first six months after delivery, a fault is assumed to have been there from the start unless shown otherwise. After that you can still claim for a fault that was there at delivery for up to six years (five in Scotland).
  • 1-year warranty: on top of these rights, if a fault develops in normal use within the first year, I'll repair or replace it free of charge — contact me to arrange it.

The warranty doesn't cover accidental damage (drops, crushing, impacts), water or mud getting in beyond what the device is built to withstand, damage from opening the case or modifying the device, or normal wear such as the battery gradually holding less charge. None of this limits your legal rights above.

Often a fault is something I can sort out remotely — a firmware update or recalibration — so email me first and we'll work out the quickest fix.

Who you're buying from

DiscoX is run by Brendan Hall, trading as DiscoX, 34 Ormonde Road, Chester, Cheshire CH2 2AH, United Kingdom. Email info@discox.co.uk. Payments are processed by Stripe; I never see or store your card details.

This policy doesn't affect your statutory rights. If you live outside the UK, your local consumer law may give you further rights, and those still apply.
